Wellspring Grants

Writing support for the work your nonprofit already does.

Wellspring Grants helps small nonprofit teams prepare a specific grant application without an ongoing consulting engagement.

A focused service

We work from the opportunity you select and the organization, project and budget information you provide. Our current service focuses on foundation, community foundation and corporate giving applications.

The model is simple: one application, a $450 fee, technology-assisted drafting and human review before delivery. Your organization keeps responsibility for its facts, eligibility, final approval and submission.

Judge the work before buying.

Our annotated demonstration uses a fictional organization and funder. It shows writing choices and a consistent project budget without presenting invented client results as proof.

We do not promise an award or publish an unsupported success rate. Funding decisions belong to funders.

A human remains part of delivery.

The service includes human review before a document is released to a customer. Our process page explains the role of technology and the review checks; the editorial score is an internal check, not a prediction of grant success.

Reach the service directly.

Have a practical question about scope, your deadline or the document you need? Use the grant-fit inquiry or email hello@wellspringgrants.com.
