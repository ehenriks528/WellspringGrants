Wellspring Grants

Answers before you start.

Understand the scope, process and practical details before purchasing writing support.

What do I get for $450?

A funder-targeted written application, human editorial review and an editable Google Doc or Word document. Revision support is available by email. Your team checks the facts, prepares required attachments and submits. Review the full scope.

Do I need to find a grant first?

The current paid service starts with the opportunity you select. Intake asks for the funder, program and guidelines. If no opportunity is selected, ask about fit before buying; an inquiry does not promise a grant match.

Which grants do you write?

Our focus is foundation, community foundation and corporate giving applications for small nonprofits. Federal applications are not currently supported. Ask before paying if the grant type or application scope is unusual.

How does two-business-day delivery work?

Delivery takes two business days after payment and complete intake. Business days are Monday–Friday. Orders outside 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern start at 9 a.m. on the next business day. A Friday-evening order starts Monday morning for Wednesday-morning writing delivery. The free inquiry does not start the clock.

What if my deadline is very soon?

Check fit before paying. Share the exact cutoff and timezone, and leave time for your own review, attachments and portal submission. A writing turnaround is not a guarantee that a last-minute application can be submitted.

When do I pay?

After completing the full intake, you continue to Stripe Checkout. The standard fee is $450 USD per application. A fit inquiry is free. Read pricing and scope before starting.

What if the draft needs changes?

One revision round is included within 14 days of delivery. Email hello@wellspringgrants.com with the changes. Additional revision rounds cost $50 each, arranged separately. A new funder or substantially different project may change the scope; check before assuming it is a revision.

How do cancellation, refunds or invoice requests work?

Orders cannot be canceled or refunded after payment has been completed. Review scope and timing before paying. For invoice or procurement questions, email hello@wellspringgrants.com before purchase.

Do you use AI or other technology?

Yes. Wellspring uses technology-assisted drafting, including Anthropic, and human review before delivery. The internal workflow reviews funder alignment, narrative, evidence, project logic, budget and specificity. Read how drafting and review work and how information is processed.

What if my funder restricts AI-assisted writing?

Contact us before purchasing. Share the exact restriction so the service can assess whether the work is appropriate. Do not assume every funder permits the same assistance, or that a human-only alternative is offered.

Who reviews the work?

The service includes human review before customer delivery. The internal editorial score helps identify issues; it is not an independent certification or a prediction of funding. For questions about reviewer experience or a specific requirement, contact Wellspring.

Do you submit the application for us?

No. We deliver the written application. Your nonprofit reviews it, prepares required forms and attachments, obtains any internal approval and submits directly to the funder.

Do you guarantee that we will win a grant?

No. The funder makes the award decision. Eligibility, program fit, priorities, competition and available funding affect the outcome. Writing support does not guarantee selection.

What should I prepare before starting?

Prepare guidelines and actual questions, the deadline, organization details, project activities, people served, outcomes, request amount and budget information. Use the free application checklist. You do not need polished prose; you do need accurate facts.

Can a new nonprofit use the service?

First confirm the funder accepts your organization and its experience level. Be honest about completed work versus plans; do not invent a track record. If the application or nonprofit status is uncertain, check fit before paying.

What about pending 501(c)(3) status or a fiscal sponsor?

Eligibility depends on the particular funder's rules and the arrangement. Confirm the opportunity accepts your status or sponsor, then check service fit before purchasing. A writing service cannot establish tax-exempt status or override eligibility requirements.

Do you prepare budgets and attachments?

A budget narrative is part of the writing offer. Do not assume financial-statement preparation, a technical budget workbook or attachment collection is included. Share those requirements through the fit inquiry before purchasing.

How will you know the funder's questions and word limits?

Provide the current guidelines and paste exact prompts, headings and limits into the intake requirements field. If the questions are behind a portal login, copy the instructions rather than sending passwords. A general foundation page may not contain the full application.

Can I see an example before paying?

Yes. The annotated sample is ungated and uses a fictional organization and funder. It illustrates the writing approach; it is not a funded client grant or a customer testimonial.

How is my information handled?

The privacy notice explains collection, service providers and contact requests. Do not send beneficiary records, passwords, bank information or card details in intake. Payment card details are collected by Stripe.

Can my board or colleague review the application?

The deliverable is an editable document for your organization's internal review. Check the actual document permissions before sharing it with another recipient. Your organization retains responsibility for final approval.

Can you help with another application later?

Each application is a separate purchase under the current offer. Every opportunity has its own questions and requirements; do not assume a prior proposal can be submitted unchanged. Check the next opportunity when you are ready.

Can I save intake and finish later?

Yes. Choose Save draft on the full intake to store an encrypted server copy for seven days. Return to the same page in the same browser and choose Resume. Saving does not create an order or reserve a slot. You can delete the saved copy. Avoid saving on shared devices; see saved-draft privacy details.

Will you make up facts to fill a gap?

The writing rules prohibit invented statistics, figures or facts. Supply accurate program and budget information. Missing or unclear information may need your input; a polished claim is not a substitute for evidence. The fictional sample shows how unresolved facts are made visible.
