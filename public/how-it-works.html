Wellspring Grants

A clear path from opportunity to application.

Use a quick fit inquiry if you need guidance. If you are ready to order, the full intake and payment route remains available.

1. Start with the opportunity.

You select a foundation, community foundation or corporate giving opportunity and review its eligibility rules. Gather the guidelines, actual application questions, word limits, deadline and required attachments.

If you are unsure about scope, have not selected a grant, have pending status or face a short deadline, check fit before paying. An inquiry is reviewed by email; it is not instant approval or a promise of a funder match.

2. Prepare and complete intake.

Tell us about your organization, program, people served, planned outcomes and budget. Use the preparation checklist to avoid gathering those details halfway through.

Paste the funder's actual prompts and instructions in the requirements field, especially if the application is inside a login-protected portal. Never send portal passwords or identifiable beneficiary records.

3. Pay for the application.

The standard fee is $450 USD, collected through Stripe after intake. Your free fit inquiry does not start the two-business-day writing turnaround. The turnaround starts after payment and complete intake. Business days are Monday–Friday; orders outside 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern start at 9 a.m. the next business day. A Friday-evening order starts Monday morning for Wednesday-morning writing delivery. Confirm feasibility before paying if the funder deadline is close.

4. Drafting and review.

The service uses technology-assisted drafting followed by human review. The writing workflow checks funder alignment, narrative strength, evidence, project logic, budget quality and specificity to your organization.

If information is missing or a draft needs client clarification, additional input may be required. Read about the drafting and review process.

5. Review, attach and submit.

You receive an editable Google Doc or Word document. Check every fact and figure, confirm the funder requirements, prepare required attachments, and obtain any board or staff approval. Your organization submits the application directly.

One revision round is included within 14 days of delivery. Additional rounds cost $50 each, arranged through hello@wellspringgrants.com. A new funder or substantially different project may change the scope; check before assuming it is a revision.

Plan backward from the funder's exact cutoff. Writing delivery, your review, required signatures, attachments and portal submission are separate steps.
