Check unusual scope before purchasing.

Foundation, community foundation and corporate giving applications are our focus. Federal applications are not currently supported. Opportunity research, financial statements, portal entry and post-award reporting should not be assumed to be included.

If the application includes many supplementary forms, a letter of inquiry, a technical budget workbook, or requirements you cannot access, use the fit inquiry to confirm the work before checkout.

Payment, timing and changes

Payment is collected through Stripe after you complete intake. The standard price is $450 USD. Any valid complimentary code is checked by the server; the checkout determines the final amount.

Business days are Monday–Friday. Orders received outside 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern start at 9 a.m. on the next business day. A Friday-evening order starts Monday morning; writing delivery is due Wednesday morning. Weekends do not count.

The fit inquiry is free and does not start the writing clock. Missing information may need clarification. Leave time for your team's review, attachments and portal submission.

One revision round is included within 14 days of delivery. Additional revision rounds cost $50 each; email hello@wellspringgrants.com to arrange them. Additional rounds are not billed automatically.

Orders cannot be canceled or refunded after payment has been completed. Review the scope and ask any procurement or invoice questions before paying.

Writing support is not a funding guarantee. The funder makes the award decision. A well-written application cannot establish eligibility or guarantee selection.
