Privacy notice

Effective October 19, 2026

What you share

Fit inquiries collect your name, email, nonprofit details, project description, optional grant link and deadline. Paid intake collects the additional organization and application details requested on that form. Do not send beneficiary records, passwords, payment card numbers, or banking information in these forms.

How we use it

We use your information to answer your inquiry, assess service fit, prepare a purchased grant application, provide support, and keep business records. Inquiry consent permits replies about that request; it does not enroll you in general marketing. We do not sell your information.

Service providers

The application uses Railway for hosting and database storage, Resend for transactional email, Stripe for payment processing, Anthropic for technology-assisted application drafting, and Google Docs/Drive for document preparation and delivery. Relevant intake information is processed by those providers to deliver the service. Stripe collects card information directly; our forms do not collect it.

Measurement and security

We record saved inquiries, checkout creation, and Stripe-confirmed payments. An essential, first-party cookie associates an inquiry with a later checkout for up to 30 days. We do not add advertising pixels or email-open tracking through this funnel. Administrative records require authentication. No internet service can promise absolute security.

Retention and requests

We retain inquiry and application records while needed to respond, deliver and support the service, and meet business or legal recordkeeping obligations. To request access, correction, deletion, or an end to contact, email hello@wellspringgrants.com. We will review your request and explain any records we must retain.

Public professional contacts

We may review publicly listed nonprofit business contacts for relevant service outreach. Such messages require human approval. You can ask us to stop by replying “stop”; we retain the minimum suppression record needed to respect that request.
