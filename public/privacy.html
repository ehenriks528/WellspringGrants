Privacy notice

Effective October 19, 2026. Contact hello@wellspringgrants.com with access, correction, deletion or contact-preference requests.

What you share

Fit inquiries collect your name, email, nonprofit details, project description, optional grant link and deadline. Paid intake collects the additional organization and application details shown on that form. Do not send identifiable beneficiary records, passwords, payment card numbers or banking details through these forms.

How the information is used

We use your information to answer your inquiry, assess service fit, prepare a purchased application, provide support and keep business records. Agreeing to receive a reply about an inquiry does not subscribe you to general marketing. We do not sell your information.

Service providers

The service uses Railway for hosting and database storage, Resend for transactional email, Stripe for payments, Anthropic for technology-assisted drafting, and Google Docs/Drive for document preparation and delivery. Relevant information is processed by those providers to deliver the service. Stripe collects card details directly; our forms do not collect them.

Optional saved intake drafts

When you choose Save draft, we store an encrypted copy of the entered application fields on the server. An HttpOnly first-party cookie authorizes access to that saved copy in the same browser. The application answers are not saved in persistent browser storage, and no resume token is placed in the page URL.

The copy expires seven days after it is first saved. Expired copies cannot be resumed and are removed by periodic cleanup, scheduled every six hours while the application is running and again at startup. You can choose Delete saved copy to remove it sooner. A change in the application's encryption configuration may make an older draft unavailable.

Saving does not create an order, subscribe you to email, or reserve a delivery slot. Avoid saving on a shared device. Saved drafts do not include payment-card information or promotional-code values.

First-party measurement

We record saved inquiries, checkout creation and Stripe-confirmed payments. An existing first-party inquiry cookie associates an inquiry with a later checkout in the same browser for up to 30 days.

We also count selected public-page visits and intake steps through a first-party endpoint. These events use an allowlist of page categories and event names. They do not contain intake answers, contact information, financial records, saved-draft tokens or full referring URLs. A random event reference prevents duplicate counts; it is not stored as a persistent visitor identifier. Client-reported activity is used for funnel estimates, not proof of revenue.

This funnel does not add advertising pixels, email-open tracking or third-party marketing analytics.

Access and retention

Administrative records require authentication. Optional saved drafts use the expiration described above. Inquiry and purchased-application records are retained while needed to respond, deliver and support the service and meet applicable business recordkeeping needs. No internet service can promise absolute security.

To request access, correction, deletion or an end to contact, email hello@wellspringgrants.com. We review requests and explain any records that must be retained.

Public professional contacts

We may review publicly listed nonprofit business contacts for relevant service outreach. Such messages require human approval. You can ask us to stop by replying “stop”; the minimum suppression record is retained to respect that request.
