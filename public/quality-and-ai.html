How drafting works

Wellspring uses Anthropic's technology to assist application drafting from your intake and available funder guidelines. The workflow scores and revises the draft before it reaches human review.

The six internal review dimensions are funder alignment, narrative strength, evidence and specificity, logic-model completeness, budget quality and differentiation. The score is an editorial tool, not an independent accreditation or a funding probability.

What human review adds

A human reviewer checks the application before customer delivery. The workflow can flag an application for more client information rather than release an underdeveloped draft. If a required fact is unavailable, it needs clarification rather than an invented substitute.

  • Do the facts and amounts match the information supplied?
  • Does the narrative address the funder's stated priorities and supplied instructions?
  • Are there unsupported figures, vague results or unresolved questions?
  • Does the written application need additional client input before release?

Your factual inputs matter.

Provide actual program results, planned activities, budget figures and evidence you can substantiate. Distinguish achieved results from future goals. Do not invent a beneficiary story to make an application sound persuasive.

When guidelines sit behind a login, paste the actual questions and limits into intake. Do not provide passwords. A guidelines link by itself may not expose every application requirement.

Check the funder's technology rules.

Funders may specify rules about outside assistance, AI use or disclosure. If your opportunity restricts technology-assisted drafting, contact us before purchasing. Do not assume this service meets a restriction without confirmation.

Know where information goes.

Relevant intake information is processed by service providers to prepare and deliver the application. See the privacy notice for the actual providers and uses. We do not claim that technology use is undetectable or appropriate for every funder.
