Use this before writing, hiring a writer, or completing intake. The funder's current instructions always take precedence.

Wellspring Grants editorial resource · October 19, 2026

You do not need polished prose before requesting writing support. You do need accurate source information. This checklist separates the facts needed to write from the final steps needed to submit.

Checks stay on this page only. No organization information is collected by this checklist.

Before writing: confirm the opportunity.

Prepare the source facts.

Map a question to its evidence.

Fictional prompt: “What will the grant pay for?” Maximum 120 words.

Source facts: Facilitator costs $8,000; materials $2,000; transport support $2,000. Total request $12,000.

Writing task: Explain each use in plain language and reconcile the request to the same budget. Do not add a new cost or claim that another donor is committed unless documented.

Before submission: finish the whole application.
