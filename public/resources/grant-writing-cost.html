A low fee and a comprehensive engagement may buy very different things. Start by defining the task you need completed.

Wellspring Grants editorial resource · October 19, 2026

A useful grant-writing quote should answer more than “How much?” It should explain who finds the opportunity, who writes, who verifies facts and budgets, what changes are included, and who submits. Without those boundaries, comparing prices can create the wrong expectation.

Three common ways to buy help

Hourly support

You pay for time. Clarify the estimated hours, what happens if scope grows, and whether meetings, research and revisions count. An hourly rate alone does not establish the likely total.

A defined application project

You pay for an agreed deliverable. Define the funder, application requirements, source material, review process and delivery conditions before work begins. An application with a short narrative and one with multiple supplementary forms may require different work.

Ongoing service

A recurring engagement may cover multiple applications, research, a grant calendar or reporting. Check the included volume and capacity, exclusions and cancellation terms. A monthly fee should not be assumed to mean unlimited work.

A scope comparison worksheet

Questions to ask each provider
WorkWhat to establish
Opportunity researchDo you bring the funder, or does the provider identify and screen opportunities?
WritingDoes the deliverable answer the actual funder prompts and limits, or provide a reusable narrative?
BudgetDoes the fee cover a narrative, spreadsheet preparation, calculations or professional financial review?
ReviewWho checks the facts and final document? What happens when information is missing?
RevisionsWhich changes, rounds and request windows are included?
SubmissionWho prepares attachments, secures signatures and completes portal entry?
TimingWhat starts the clock, and how do missing inputs or changed instructions affect it?
PaymentWhen is payment collected, and what cancellation or refund rules apply?

Federal applications are outside our current service. Check unusual scope and near-term deadlines before purchasing. Read the full pricing and responsibilities page rather than treating this guide as a complete quote.

Compare with doing it yourself.

A template or self-service AI tool can assist a team that has time to write and review. A delivered writing service buys outside capacity. Neither removes the need for accurate program information or final organizational approval.

Do not justify a fee by assuming the grant will be won. Compare the writing task and team capacity first. The funder retains the award decision.
