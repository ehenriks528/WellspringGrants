When outside writing help is useful

An executive director may know the program in detail but have little uninterrupted writing time. A development employee may need capacity for a specific deadline. A board volunteer may need a defined service they can explain and approve.

Wellspring's current service addresses that writing task for a $450 per-application fee. It does not replace opportunity selection, financial preparation or the nonprofit's relationship with the funder.

Start with the actual application.

Foundation and corporate applications do not all ask the same questions. Share the program guidelines, question wording, required headings, word limits and attachment list. The more specific the source material, the clearer the writing brief.

Common narrative topics include organizational background, need, project description, outcomes, evaluation, budget and sustainability. Those topics help prepare information; they are not a universal substitute for a funder's application form.

Scope at a glance

  • Foundation, community foundation and corporate giving applications.
  • Writing based on your organization's facts and selected funder.
  • Technology-assisted drafting with human review.
  • Google Doc or Word delivery and one revision round within 14 days of delivery.
  • Your team reviews, adds required attachments and submits.

Federal applications are not currently supported. Ask about unusual forms, technical budget requirements, pending status and short deadlines before buying.

Send a brief your team can approve.

For a useful fit review, identify the funder and program, the exact deadline with timezone, and the project you want to fund. Note the actual application questions, word limits, attachments and any existing draft. If instructions sit behind a login, copy the requirements rather than sending passwords.

Keep beneficiary information anonymous and distinguish documented results from future goals. If key facts or financial records are missing, tell us what still needs confirmation. The application checklist explains what to gather for full intake.

Use the free inquiry to check scope before payment. It does not start the two-business-day delivery clock or guarantee an award. For the fee, revision window and responsibilities, review pricing and scope. You can also inspect the clearly labeled fictional sample and read how AI assistance and human review work.
