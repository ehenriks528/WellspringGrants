Wellspring Grants

Service scope and purchase information.

Read this alongside the pricing page before placing an application-writing order.

The purchased service

The standard offer is one funder-targeted written grant application for $450 USD, with technology-assisted drafting, human review and editable Google Doc or Word delivery. Foundation, community foundation and corporate giving applications are the current focus. Federal applications are not currently supported.

Your organization's responsibilities

You select the opportunity, confirm its eligibility rules and supply accurate facts and instructions. Your organization reviews the delivered application, prepares required attachments, obtains internal approval and submits directly to the funder. Do not provide passwords or identifiable beneficiary records.

Payment and timing

Payment is collected through Stripe after the full intake. The advertised writing turnaround is two business days after payment and complete intake. Business days are Monday–Friday. Orders outside 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern start at 9 a.m. on the next business day; weekends do not count. A free inquiry does not start that clock. Confirm close deadlines or unusual application scope before paying, and leave time for your review and submission.

Review, changes and billing questions

One revision round is included within 14 days of delivery. Additional revision rounds cost $50 each and are arranged through hello@wellspringgrants.com; they are not billed automatically.

Orders cannot be canceled or refunded after payment has been completed. Confirm scope, feasibility and any invoice or procurement requirements before paying.

Funding decisions

Writing support does not guarantee an award. The funder decides whether to accept and fund an application.

Information and technology

Read the privacy notice and technology and review explanation. If the funder restricts outside or AI-assisted writing, contact Wellspring before purchasing.
